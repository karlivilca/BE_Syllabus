Business English Reading Comprehension

Master essential business documents with confidence

Level: A1 (True Beginners)
Format: Online, Autonomous Study
Total Units: 6

Course Objective

Main Course Aim

To enable learners to comprehend simple, short business-related texts (emails, schedules, notices, etc.) using foundational vocabulary and basic sentence structures.

Course Curriculum

Six comprehensive units designed to build your business English reading skills step by step

Unit 1

Introductions and Greetings in Business Emails

Aim: Recognize common greetings, sign-offs, and basic email structure.

  • Key phrases (e.g., "Dear Mr./Ms.," "Best regards")
  • Email components (subject line, body)
  • Write a 3-sentence email introducing yourself

Unit 2

Understanding Company Profiles

Aim: Identify basic company information (name, location, services).

  • Vocabulary: "company," "employees," "products"
  • True/False questions about company profiles
  • Create a 5-sentence profile for a fictional company

Unit 3

Reading Schedules and Appointments

Aim: Extract key details from calendars and schedules.

  • Days, times, and phrases like "meeting at 10:00 AM"
  • Answering questions about a weekly schedule
  • Write your daily work schedule using a template

Unit 4

Simple Product Descriptions

Aim: Understand basic product features.

  • Vocabulary: "price," "color," "size," "buy," "order"
  • Matching products to descriptions
  • Describe an everyday office product in 4 sentences

Unit 5

Workplace Notices and Instructions

Aim: Follow short instructions.

  • Imperatives: "Do not enter," "Please sign here"
  • Sequencing steps (e.g., "How to use the printer")
  • Write a 3-step instruction for an office tool

Unit 6

Basic Business Vocabulary in Context

Aim: Recognize common business terms.

  • Review of key vocabulary from Units 1–5
  • Crossword puzzles; categorizing words
  • Read a short business memo and highlight key terms

Course Structure

Each unit follows the same effective learning pattern

Introduction

20–30 minutes

Short text/vocabulary list with audio support

Practice

45–60 minutes

Interactive exercises (drag-and-drop, multiple-choice, matching)

Task

30–45 minutes

Writing or reading-based activity with downloadable template

Progress Tracking

Self-Assessment

End-of-unit checklist to track your learning progress:

  • "I can understand simple emails"
  • "I can read a schedule"
  • "I can follow basic workplace instructions"

Final Review

Unit 6 includes a comprehensive quiz to reinforce retention:

  • Review of key vocabulary from all units
  • Practical reading comprehension exercises
  • Self-evaluation of learning outcomes
